# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women (primarily) and men in San Luis Potosí, México — ages 20s–50s, seeking self-care, stress relief, or body treatments. They arrive in a treat-yourself mindset: deliberate, emotionally invested, willing to pay for quality. Context is mobile-first: discovering the spa on Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google Maps and visiting the site to decide whether to book. There's a growing men's segment (Men's Recovery Month) but the core audience is women.

## Product Purpose

Drive bookings via Fresha and WhatsApp. The site must move a visitor from discovery → trust → reservation in the fewest steps. Supporting goals: showcase services and promotions, surface real reviews and before/after evidence, and give instant answers via the AI chat assistant. Success = a tap on "Reservar" or a WhatsApp message sent.

## Brand Personality

Cálido · Premium · Cercano — warm, elevated, approachable. The spa is a trusted luxury friend, not a clinical brand or a flashy social-media account. Confident without being cold, refined without being distant.

## Anti-references

- **Chain pharmacy beauty brands** — mass-market, generic, zero soul.
- **Oversaturated Instagram aesthetics** — neon glow, heavy gradients, loud filters.
- **Corporate medical clinic** — sterile whites, cold blues, clinical detachment.
- **Generic luxury template** — "dark background + gold serif" cookie-cutter luxury.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth is structural, not decorative** — care and intimacy should come through layout and hierarchy, not just palette.
2. **Trust before transaction** — real reviews, real therapist names, real results before asking for a booking.
3. **Local and specific** — SLP identity, named therapists, real promotions. Not abstractions that could belong to any spa anywhere.
4. **Premium without pretension** — elevated enough to justify the price; approachable enough that no one feels excluded.
5. **Mobile-first conversion** — WhatsApp and Fresha are the money buttons. Every screen must make reaching them effortless.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Keyboard-navigable interactive elements. Meaningful alt text on service and before/after images. Reduced-motion honored for all animations.
